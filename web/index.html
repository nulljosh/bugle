One call, everyone hears it

One press, every feed

A bugle calls everyone in at once. Bugle does that for the feeds you follow: every story, from every outlet, in one list.

Subscribe

Any site, any feed

Paste a site address and Bugle finds its feed. Newsrooms, tech and Mac sites and ten cities' local desks are a tap away, and OPML brings the rest.

Timeline

One story, every newsroom

The same story from several outlets is one row, with how left, center and right covered it. Blindspots shows what one side skipped, Popular Left and Popular Right what each side covers most, and Both Sides the stories to compare.

Reading

The whole story, quietly

Stories open in full even when a feed only sends a summary, with sharp pictures and the days of coverage before. Hacker News stories come with the whole comment thread. A dot marks what you have not read, and a compact layout fits more on a screen.

Listen

Podcasts beside the news

Your shows sit on a shelf beside the headlines, and each episode opens on its cover. Play at up to 2x, line up the next ones and see how long they run, download for the plane, and set a sleep timer. The Briefing reads you the day's five biggest stories.

Start

Pick what you follow

The first launch asks what you care about, AI, housing, your city, and makes each one a chip on Home.

Offline

Keeps what you save

Saved stories open with their pictures on the train.

Private

No account needed

Read without signing up, in nineteen languages, and translate any story on your device. An optional account syncs your feeds, saved stories, topics and mutes.

Native

At home on Apple devices

A widget, Siri, Spotlight, Share from Safari, and three columns on iPad and Mac.
