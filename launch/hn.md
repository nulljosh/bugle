Show HN: Bugle – Every feed you follow, in one clean list

I follow forty blogs and I was reading none of them, because they were scattered across tabs and newsletters. Bugle is the plain version of a feed reader. Add a URL, entries show up in order, read ones cache on your Mac. No account, no algorithm deciding what you see, nothing phoned home. $0.99 on the App Store, free on the web at bugle.jaybulb.com, source on GitHub.
